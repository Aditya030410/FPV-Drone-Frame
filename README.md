                                                  FPV Drone Frame
What is it ?
A FPV Drone Frame !!! It is a drone frame for FPV drones made by me !!! in fusion 360 !!, it has 2 floors and holes for screws and free spaces which will work as the central hub to insert controller for the flights and other electronics components which will help the drone actually fly !!

Why I made it !?
Actually, I am getting interested into drones, hardware and stuff and i have always to make a drone by MYSELF !! so as  a start, I had to do something, so I started with CAD, and i actually made my frist DRONE FRAME !! I am looking forward to learn more about fusion 360 and continue my journey of making a drone !!!

How I made it !?
I am VERY new to CAD i just a build a Phone stand in Onshape then a Drone Propeller... it was very cool, check it out here : https://www.printables.com/model/1852385-drone-propeller 
Then I always thought of CADing my Drone Frame SO as i told u i am new to CAD i watched tutorial from youtube about the same : https://youtu.be/DoC8GQmAyYM
Big thanks to the creator, It was a very good tutorial and very helpful !! In the video It was only shown how to make the lower body and arms but by taking an inspiration from the thumbnail, i also made the upper body and some other minor changess !!

p.s. - i also put the drone propellers i made in the model to make it look cool lol !!
